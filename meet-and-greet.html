3 Ask Ms. Colletti

The doors — the big one

  • Which door do we use for 8:45 drop-off? He's in the Modular Building. Door 1 on McLean is the main building side entrance, used for 5 PM after-school pickup — probably not his morning door. The principal's opening-day letter was meant to cover this and hasn't arrived.
  • Where exactly do I leave him — at the door, or the classroom? Kindergarten drop-off is usually different from older grades, and it may change after the first week.
  • Which door for 3:45 dismissal, and where do I stand?
  • Is the 7:30 Apollo drop-off door different again? Before-care may use a separate entrance from the regular morning one.

The A–F rotation

  • Which subject falls on each letter day for Room 002B? Pulaski publishes the rotation calendar but leaves the letter-to-subject fields blank for each homeroom. Without this you can't know which day is PE or library. Monday Aug 24 is Day A. A:   B:   C:   D:   E:   F:
  • Does he need anything specific on particular letter days? Gym shoes for PE, a bag for library books, anything for music or technology.

The school day

  • What time does your class eat lunch? The first block starts recess at 10:00 and eats at 10:30. If that's his block, breakfast matters and he'll be hungry long before 3:45.
  • Does he need a morning snack from home, and is the classroom nut-free? SMA requires a nut-free snack for after school; the classroom policy may be separate.
  • Is there a rest or quiet time in the day?
  • Inside shoes — what kind, where do they live, should they be labelled? Required for your room specifically. Worth knowing whether they need to be slip-on for a five-year-old.
  • Should the supplies be labelled with his name, or do they go into a shared pool?
  • Where does his water bottle live, and can he refill it? The water fountains in both buildings were replaced over the summer, so they should be working.
  • What does a typical first week look like? Helps you tell him what's coming, which is most of what settles a nervous five-year-old.

Staying in touch

  • How do you prefer to be contacted, and what's a realistic reply time?
  • Is there a classroom app or newsletter we should be on? Seesaw, ClassDojo, Remind — whatever she uses. Worth signing up on the spot.
  • How will you let us know how he's settling in? Curriculum Night isn't until Sep 10, and the first progress report is Sep 24. That's a long first month with no scheduled signal.
  • What should we do if he's having a hard time with the transition?
  • How do we report an absence or a late arrival?
  • Is there a room parent or class contact list?

None of the three school emails, the supply list, or the registration confirmations mention this at all. 5 Apollo and ChicagoSMA

Both operate on site, so this is your chance to get a real person rather than an 800-number.

Who to talk to

  • Who is the ChicagoSMA on-site manager? Their email says on-site managers coordinate all classes and students.

The handoff — walk it through with them

  • Confirm: SMA collects him from Room 002B at 3:45, Mon–Thu? Their email says coordinators collect Modular Building students from their classroom, then take them to the main cafeteria for snack. Confirm that's how it works for a kindergartner.
  • Confirm: SMA walks him back to Apollo at 5:00, rather than releasing him? Their policy is to escort Apollo children back to aftercare — but only if they know he's one. Make sure they have him flagged.
  • Fridays: who brings him from Room 002B to Apollo at 3:45? The one genuine gap. No SMA class on Fridays, and nothing in the Apollo confirmation says whether they collect from modular classrooms. Don't leave without an answer.
  • Does Apollo provide the afternoon snack, or should he bring one?
  • Does he need a change of clothes or anything else at Apollo? The Apollo confirmation is purely a receipt — no packing list at all.
  • Which door and what's the routine for 7:30 AM before-care?
  • What happens if we're late past 6:00, and what does it cost?
  • Does Apollo run on school days off? 34 weekdays off this year, starting with Sep 7 and the Friday Sep 25 PD day. Those need a plan either way.
